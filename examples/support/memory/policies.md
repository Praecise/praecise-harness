# Acme support policies

## Refunds
Refunds are available within 30 days of delivery. Once approved, the money
returns to the original payment method in five business days. Items marked
final sale cannot be refunded.

## Shipping
Standard shipping takes three to five business days. Express takes one.
Orders placed after 2pm local time ship the next business day.

## Accounts
A customer can change their email themselves from Settings. Deleting an
account is permanent and takes 24 hours to process.
